This mod adds 6 talent branches:
1. Warrior
2. Ranger
3. Mage
4. Summoner
5. Warlock
6. Traveler

Complete tasks and level up to earn talent points for talent trees.

Traveler talents will give you the ability to explore the world more efficiently and quickly.

The Warlock is a reworked version of the Mage. It will require you to keep your health low to maximize damage.
