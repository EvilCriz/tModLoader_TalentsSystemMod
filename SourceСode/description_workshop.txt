This mod adds 6 talent branches:
1. Warrior
2. Ranger
3. Mage
4. Summoner
5. Warlock
6. Traveler

--------------------

Complete quests to increase your level
For each level, you receive 1 talent point for EACH branch
The list of quests can be found in the tab below

When completing tasks, some of them count for all players on the server
General quests are marked with the symbol: ⟳
Local quests are marked with the symbol: ⟲

Some operations are registered with a delay of a couple of seconds (there may be a delay of up to 15 seconds on the server)
Some tasks have been replaced for the Calamity Mod

--------------------

Only 1 talent branch can be active at a time
You can change the active talent branch at any time
You can reset and redistribute your talents at any time

A set of talent tasks for the Calamity Mod

--------------------

The Warlock branch is a reworked version of the Mage mechanic
You will need to keep your health low to increase your damage.

--------------------

The mod defines configuration settings.
They allow you to customize the main menu controls.
